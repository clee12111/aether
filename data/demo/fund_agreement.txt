HORIZON GROWTH PARTNERS FUND I, LP
LIMITED PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT — DISTRIBUTION AND ALLOCATION PROVISIONS
Effective Date: January 1, 2024

ARTICLE IV — DISTRIBUTIONS AND ALLOCATIONS

Section 4.1 — Pro-Rata Distribution Requirement
All distributions of Available Cash shall be made to the Partners pro-rata in
accordance with their respective Ownership Percentages as set forth in
Schedule A, unless the General Partner has approved an exception in writing
pursuant to Section 4.5. No distribution shall be made on a non-pro-rata
basis absent such written approval, which must specify the business
rationale and be countersigned by the Fund's independent auditor.

Section 4.2 — Quarterly Distribution Cap
No single Limited Partner shall receive, in any fiscal quarter, distributions
exceeding twenty-five percent (25%) of the total distributions made to all
Partners during such quarter. Any amount that would exceed this cap shall be
retained in the Partner's capital account and distributed in the following
quarter, subject to the same limitation. The General Partner shall monitor
compliance with this cap on a quarterly basis.

Section 4.3 — Small-Holder Distribution Cap
Any Partner whose Ownership Percentage is below fifteen percent (15%) shall
be subject to an additional distribution limitation: such Partner's
distributions in any quarter shall not exceed one and one-half times (1.5x)
the product of (a) such Partner's Ownership Percentage and (b) the total
distributions made during the quarter. This provision is intended to prevent
disproportionate cash flow to minority holders.

Section 4.4 — Preferred Return Allocation
Prior to any profit-sharing distributions under Section 4.6, the Fund shall
allocate a preferred return of eight percent (8%) per annum on each
Partner's beginning capital balance for the applicable period. The preferred
return shall accrue and be allocated on a quarterly basis (2% per quarter)
and shall be satisfied before any performance-based or incentive
distributions are calculated. Unpaid preferred return shall accumulate and
compound quarterly.

Section 4.5 — Written Exceptions
The General Partner may, in its sole discretion, authorize distributions
that deviate from the pro-rata requirement of Section 4.1 or the caps set
forth in Sections 4.2 and 4.3, provided that: (a) such authorization is
documented in a written memorandum delivered to all affected Partners no
fewer than ten (10) business days prior to the distribution date; (b) the
memorandum states the specific business rationale for the deviation; and
(c) the Fund's independent auditor has reviewed and acknowledged the
exception.

Section 4.6 — Profit Sharing
After satisfaction of the Preferred Return under Section 4.4, remaining
distributable income shall be allocated eighty percent (80%) to the Limited
Partners (pro-rata by Ownership Percentage) and twenty percent (20%) to the
General Partner as carried interest, subject to any applicable clawback
provisions under Article VII.

ARTICLE V — CAPITAL ACCOUNTS AND OWNERSHIP

Section 5.1 — Ownership Percentage Integrity
The aggregate of all Partners' Ownership Percentages shall at all times
equal one hundred percent (100%), with an allowable tolerance of plus or
minus one-half of one percent (+/- 0.5%). If the aggregate Ownership
Percentages deviate beyond this tolerance at any quarter-end, the General
Partner shall within five (5) business days (a) identify the source of the
discrepancy, (b) adjust the Ownership Percentages to restore compliance,
and (c) notify all Partners in writing of the adjustment.

Section 5.2 — Capital Account Maintenance
Each Partner's capital account shall be maintained in accordance with
Treasury Regulation Section 1.704-1(b)(2)(iv) and shall be increased by
contributions and income allocations and decreased by distributions and
loss allocations. The Fund Administrator shall reconcile capital accounts
no less frequently than quarterly.
