HORIZON WEALTH ADVISORS, LLC
REGISTERED INVESTMENT ADVISER — COMPLIANCE POLICIES AND PROCEDURES
Policy Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Last Reviewed: March 15, 2024

SECTION 1 — PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 This Compliance Manual establishes the policies and procedures
governing the investment advisory activities of Horizon Wealth Advisors,
LLC ("the Firm"), a registered investment adviser under the Investment
Advisers Act of 1940. These policies apply to all advisory personnel,
supervised persons, and associated persons of the Firm.

1.2 The Chief Compliance Officer ("CCO") is responsible for the
administration, interpretation, and enforcement of these policies and
shall conduct no fewer than one annual review of their adequacy and
effectiveness pursuant to Rule 206(4)-7.

SECTION 2 — TRANSACTION MONITORING

2.1 All client account transactions shall be subject to automated
surveillance. Any single transaction with a gross value exceeding One
Hundred Thousand Dollars ($100,000) shall be automatically flagged for
review by the Compliance Department.

2.2 Flagged transactions shall be classified as follows:
    (a) Level 1 — transactions between $100,000 and $250,000: review
        within three (3) business days;
    (b) Level 2 — transactions between $250,001 and $500,000: review
        within two (2) business days with CCO notification;
    (c) Level 3 — transactions exceeding $500,000: same-day review with
        mandatory CCO sign-off before settlement.

2.3 The Compliance Department shall maintain a transaction monitoring log
documenting the date of the flag, the reviewing officer, the disposition
(approved, escalated, or rejected), and any corrective action taken.

SECTION 3 — CONCENTRATION LIMITS

3.1 No individual client portfolio under the Firm's management shall hold
a position in any single security, fund, or issuer exceeding thirty
percent (30%) of the portfolio's total market value at the time of trade
execution.

3.2 If market appreciation causes a position to exceed the 30% threshold
after execution, the portfolio manager shall rebalance the position to
below 30% within fifteen (15) business days, unless the client has
provided a signed written waiver pursuant to Section 3.4.

3.3 Sector concentration shall not exceed fifty percent (50%) of any
client portfolio. For purposes of this section, sectors are defined by
the Global Industry Classification Standard (GICS) at the sector level.

3.4 A client may waive the concentration limits of Sections 3.1 and 3.2
by executing a Concentration Risk Acknowledgment form, which must
disclose: (a) the specific position and percentage; (b) the risks of
concentrated holdings; and (c) the client's acknowledgment that the
position deviates from the Firm's standard investment guidelines.

SECTION 4 — SUITABILITY AND BEST INTEREST

4.1 All investment recommendations and discretionary trades must be
suitable for the client based on the client's documented investment
profile, which includes: risk tolerance, investment time horizon,
liquidity needs, tax status, and stated investment objectives.

4.2 Advisory personnel shall not execute a trade that conflicts with a
client's stated risk profile without prior written client approval. Risk
profiles are categorized as: Conservative, Moderate, Growth, and
Aggressive. A trade is considered conflicting if the security's risk
classification exceeds the client's profile category by two or more
levels.

4.3 The Firm shall review client investment profiles no less frequently
than annually or upon notification of a material change in the client's
financial circumstances.

SECTION 5 — FLAGGED ITEM REVIEW AND REPORTING

5.1 All items flagged by the compliance monitoring systems described in
Sections 2 through 4 must be reviewed and dispositioned within five (5)
business days of the flag date, unless a shorter review period is
specified in the applicable section.

5.2 The CCO shall prepare a monthly Compliance Summary Report
documenting: (a) total number of flags generated; (b) number resolved
within the required timeframe; (c) number of escalations; (d) any
regulatory reporting obligations triggered; and (e) remedial actions
taken.

5.3 All compliance records, including flagged item dispositions, review
memoranda, and client waivers, shall be retained for a minimum of five
(5) years from the date of creation in accordance with Rule 204-2 of
the Investment Advisers Act.

5.4 Any pattern of repeated flags involving the same client account,
security, or advisory personnel shall be escalated to the CCO for
enhanced review. Three or more flags within a rolling sixty (60) day
period shall trigger a mandatory compliance inquiry.
